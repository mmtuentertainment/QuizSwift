## Decisions Made
1. **Separate CuratedQuestion model** - Keep ExtractedQuestion for legacy extraction, CuratedQuestion for new pipeline. Allows parallel systems during transition without data migration.
2. **Event-based routing** - pdf/curation.ready triggers curation pipeline, pdf/processing.complete triggers legacy extraction. Clean separation without conditional logic in handlers.
3. **Full metadata storage** - Store contentAnalysis, conceptExtraction, and bloomDistribution on Document for debugging and analytics visibility.
